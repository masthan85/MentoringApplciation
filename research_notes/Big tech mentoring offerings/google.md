# Google's AI mentoring, coaching, career-development and learning offerings (as of 15 Oct 2026)

Method note: research used web search snippets only. WebFetch failed with DNS errors (blog.google not resolvable), so no Google page was read in full. Every claim below is from a search-result summary, and Google primary URLs appear only where the search surfaced them. Treat as a map of what to verify. The existing landscape report (AI mentoring apps landscape 2026.md) listed Gemini memory as "Gems hold only setup context" (Android Authority, old) and said Coursera Coach and similar were unsearched. Updates and contradictions are flagged "UPDATE".

## 1. Shipped vs experimental: Career Dreamer, Grow with Google, Skills Boost, Gemini learning tools

### Takeaway
Google has no product that acts as a long-term personal mentor. It has (a) an experimental US-only career exploration tool (Career Dreamer), (b) paid or free certificate courses via Coursera, (c) a consolidated Google Skills catalogue, and (d) Gemini learning modes (Guided Learning, study notebooks) aimed mainly at students. Career Dreamer is the closest thing to career guidance, and I found no evidence it reached the UK.

### Cited Findings
- Career Dreamer: Google Labs experiment launched Feb 2025; builds a "career identity" statement, shows a visual web of careers matching your background, and lets you work with Gemini on CV/cover letters. Uses Lightcast and BLS job-market data, and points to Google Career Certificates and Cloud Skills Boost. It lists no job postings. — [TechCrunch, 19 Feb 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/19/googles-career-dreamer-uses-ai-to-help-you-explore-job-possibilities); [Ubergizmo](https://www.ubergizmo.com/2025/02/google-career-dreamer/)
- Career Dreamer availability: US only as an experiment at launch. A 2026 search found no announcement of UK or wider expansion. — [Ain.ua summary](https://en.ain.ua/2025/02/24/google-launches-career-dreamer); (absence of evidence, not confirmation)
- Guided Learning in Gemini: launched 6 Aug 2025, a Socratic mode powered by LearnLM (models fine-tuned for learning, built with educators); free to all Gemini users via the Tools menu; includes quizzes, diagrams, video. Google cited an RCT with Fab AI (1,763 Sierra Leone secondary students, 8 weeks, at least 12 hours of use), reporting a gain equivalent to moving from the 50th to the 64th percentile in maths. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/guided-learning/) (surfaced via search, not read); [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/google-launches-guided-learning-for-gemini-joining-the-ai-tutor-arms-race)
- Gemini "study notebooks": launched 25 June 2026; start with a diagnostic quiz, build lessons around gaps, track progress on a dashboard; exam-prep (JEE, NEET, ENEM, ACT, GRE being added in summer 2026); mobile rollout "later in summer 2026". Sources are shared with NotebookLM. This is the closest Google feature to adaptive progress tracking, but it is exam and student oriented. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-study-notebooks/); [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/study-notebooks-feature-gemini-app-3681541); [JustBeingResourceful, 3 Jul 2026](https://justbeingresourceful.com/2026/07/03/google-study-notebooks-in-gemini-your-ai-tutor-that-knows-what-you-need-to-learn-next/)
- NotebookLM reportedly renamed "Gemini Notebook" in July 2026 (one source says 16 July). Secondary sources only; sources disagree on exact date wording. — [Outwood EdTech](https://edtech.outwood.com/docs/ai/gemini-notebook/); [timewell.jp](https://timewell.jp/en/columns/notebooklm-update)
- Grow with Google AI Essentials: about 5 hours, on Coursera. The UK enrolment page says subscription after a 7-day free trial (quoted $49/month on the page the search returned, so the UK GBP price is unverified). Free access exists for some groups via Google.org AI Opportunity Fund partners (third-party claim; UK coverage unclear). — [Grow with Google UK enrol page](https://grow.google/intl/uk/enroll-certificates/ai-essentials-mid/); [Grow with Google AI](https://grow.google/ai)
- Grow with Google also lists an "AI Professional Certificate" (labs, portfolio); UK certificates page says enrolment includes three months of Google AI Pro. — [Grow with Google UK certificates](https://grow.google/intl/uk/google-career-certificates/)
- Google Skills (rebrand/consolidation of Cloud Skills Boost, announced around Oct 2025): about 3,000 courses from Google Cloud, DeepMind, Grow with Google and Google for Education. Gemini Code Assist in 20+ labs, new badges (e.g. ADK agent deployment), org features (assignments, leaderboards, reporting). GEAR cohort programme aims at one million developers building agents. — [Design Compass, 14 Oct 2025](https://designcompass.org/en/2025/10/14/google-skills-googles-ai-learning-platform/); [Google Developer forum](https://discuss.google.dev/t/learn-ai-and-everything-beyond-with-google-skills/279914)
- LearnLM: UK secondary-school trial (summer 2025, Eedi platform) found a supervised human+AI tutor matched a human tutor alone on fixing mistakes and misconceptions. — [Search summary of Google Research](https://research.google/blog/how-googles-ai-can-help-transform-health-professions-education/) (weakly sourced)

### Inferences
- Career Dreamer is exploration, not mentoring: no goals, no follow-up, no memory documented. Experimental label means no commitments.
- Guided Learning and study notebooks show Google can do diagnostic, adaptive, progress-tracked learning, but only for taught subjects and exams, not for open-ended career growth.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm Career Dreamer status in Oct 2026 or any UK launch.
- No UK GBP price for AI Essentials or the AI Professional Certificate confirmed.
- Coursera Coach (Coursera's own AI) was not researched; Google Skills price/tier for individuals in the UK not found.
- Gems: no source this round on whether Gems now carry persistent memory (UPDATE area, see section 3).

## 2. Workspace / Gemini for coaching or growth; Google's internal programmes; UK subscriptions

### Takeaway
Google's workplace offerings are productivity-oriented (Gemini in Workspace, Gemini Enterprise agents); education offerings are free-ish for institutions. No documented coaching product. Internal mentoring programmes (g2g) were not found in this round.

### Cited Findings
- Workspace for Education: includes Gemini for Education, NotebookLM and Gemini in Classroom at no cost per Google UK edu pages; premium "Google AI Pro for Education" gives expanded model access. Under-18 and business/enterprise accounts are excluded from some Gemini memory features. — [Google edu UK](https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_uk/workspace-for-education/add-ons/gemini-education/)
- Education data commitment (May 2024 announcement): for 18+ school accounts, data not human-reviewed or used to train models. Dated, framed as future at the time. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/classroom/google-ai-gemini-workspace-for-education/)
- Gemini Enterprise (successor to Agentspace, launched Oct 2025 per a third-party guide): Agent Gallery (Google-made, org, partner and custom agents), Agent Designer no-code builder; tiers reported as Business, Standard, Plus, Frontline. Docs list HR as an example use case but no named HR learning agent was found. — [Google Cloud docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/agent-gallery); [gosearch.ai](https://www.gosearch.ai/faqs/gemini-enterprise-pricing/) (third party, pricing unverified)
- UK subscription prices: Wise (undated) lists Google AI Pro £18.99/month and AI Ultra £234.99/month. Engadget (I/O 2026) reports Ultra now starts at $100/month (was $250) with a $200 option; no UK GBP for the new tier. Engadget also reports an AI Plus price cut to about $5/month with 400GB storage; one third-party claims UK AI Plus is £6.99. — [Wise](https://wise.com/gb/blog/google-ai-pricing); [Engadget Ultra](https://engadget.com/2176060/the-google-ai-ultra-plan-now-starts-at-100-a-month); [Engadget Plus](https://engadget.com/2190039/google-cuts-the-price-of-its-ai-plus-plan-and-doubles-the-storage); [glbgpt](https://www.glbgpt.com/hub/is-google-ai-plus-plan-available-in-the-uk-2026-price-review/) (low quality)
- UK Google Career Certificates Employer Consortium: launched 2022 with 11 employers (BBC, BT, Deloitte, John Lewis among them) who agreed to consider certificate holders; the current UK list was not retrieved. Certificates described earlier as level-4 equivalent, 3-6 months. A "150+ companies" figure appears but seems US. — [Computer Weekly](https://computerweekly.com/news/252521648/British-businesses-to-recognise-Google-skills-certificates); [UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/education/google-tech-employer-consortium-20220618); [Grow UK consortium](https://grow.google/intl/uk/employer-consortium/)
- DeepMind "AI co-clinician" initiative (30 Apr 2026, "triadic care" with physician authority) is health, not career; mentioned only as an example of Google's human-in-the-loop framing. — [Distilinfo](https://distilinfo.com/2026/05/01/google-deepmind-launches-ai-co-clinician-initiative/) (secondary)

### Inferences
- The consumer paywall for Google's best Gemini features is AI Pro (about £19/month, unverified current), the same range as ChatGPT/Claude paid plans in the existing report.

### Gaps
- Google internal mentoring/coaching programmes (g2g, etc.): not searched successfully; unknown.
- "Workspace coaching-style prompts": no source found.
- Current GBP prices for AI Pro/Plus/Ultra, Gemini Enterprise per-seat, and Google Skills individual subscription: not verified from Google pages.

## 3. Cloud reference architectures for building mentor/tutor agents

### Takeaway
Google provides strong building blocks for exactly the missing pieces (ADK, Agent Engine Sessions and Memory Bank, London region) but I found no official coaching or tutoring blueprint. Samples are explicitly unsupported.

### Cited Findings
- google/adk-samples: ready-to-use agents in Python, TypeScript, Go, Java, Kotlin, Android; includes a Memory Bank sample for cross-session memory, a human-in-the-loop Ambient Expense Agent and a Deep Search multi-agent. README states not an officially supported Google product. Star counts vary 5k-10k by snapshot. — [adk-samples README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/google/adk-samples@main/README.md); [pkg.go.dev](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/google/adk-samples)
- Vertex AI Agent Engine: Sessions and Memory Bank GA from Dec 2025 per release notes; billing for Sessions, Memory Bank and Code Execution began 28 Jan 2026; London (europe-west2) is listed for Runtime, Sessions and Memory Bank, but one overview page still says "Memory Bank (Preview)" for regions, so there is an internal doc inconsistency; data residency at rest shown as supported for Memory Bank in the feature table, but no explicit London residency guarantee found. — [Agent Builder release notes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-builder/release-notes); [Locations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-builder/locations)
- Gemini Enterprise Agent Designer allows no-code agents on a schedule (proactive follow-up potential). — [docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/agent-gallery)

### Inferences
- A UK-hosted mentor agent (ADK + Agent Engine Sessions + Memory Bank in London) is technically feasible on Google Cloud; this is my inference from the docs, not a Google claim. Goal and evidence tracking would have to be custom (e.g. in Firestore/BigQuery).

### Gaps
- No Google-published tutoring/coaching/HR learning blueprint located (may exist in Cloud Architecture Center or Agent Garden; not searched directly).
- Pricing for Memory Bank not retrieved.
- Third-party model processing residency (Gemini API in UK) not verified.

## 4. Memory, personalisation, privacy, UK residency

### Takeaway
Gemini consumer memory reached the UK in spring 2026, on by default; Personal Intelligence (cross-app) launched US-first in Jan 2026 as a paid beta. Neither provides a user-editable goal or skill model.

### Cited Findings
- UPDATE: Google's UK blog says Gemini "Memories" is available in the UK (reported 29 Apr 2026): on by default, toggle under Personal context > Memory; also tools to import memories and chat history from other AI apps (ZIP upload), rolling out over following weeks; business, enterprise and under-18 accounts not supported. — [Google UK blog](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-europe/united-kingdom/gemini-launches-new-personalisation-features-in-the-uk/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/gemini-brings-memories-and-chat-import-to-uk-users/). Contradicted by an older third-party guide saying UK excluded — [MemoryPlugin](https://blog.memoryplugin.com/how-gemini-memory-works/) (likely outdated)
- Memory depends on Keep Activity being on; Gems and Live reportedly outside it (third party). — MemoryPlugin as above
- Personal Intelligence: beta launched 14 Jan 2026 for US AI Pro/Ultra subscribers; opt-in, off by default; links Gmail, Photos, Search, YouTube; Google says it does not train directly on Gmail inbox or Photos library, and warns of inaccuracy and over-personalisation; avoids proactive assumptions about sensitive topics like health. UK availability not found. — [TechCrunch, 14 Jan 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/14/geminis-new-beta-feature-provides-proactive-responses-based-on-your-photos-emails-and-more); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/01/14/gemini-personal-intelligence/)

### Inferences
- Chat-history import means a user's prior ChatGPT/Claude history could seed Gemini; conversely there is no documented export of structured Gemini memory (not verified).

### Gaps
- Gemini Apps privacy notice details (human review, retention, 18-month defaults) not read.
- UK data residency for consumer Gemini: no claim found.
- Whether Personal Intelligence reached the UK by Oct 2026.

## 5. What Google does NOT do (gaps vs a personal long-term mentor)

### Takeaway
Based on the above, nothing Google ships combines persistent goals, evidence-based progress, a chosen mentoring persona and human handoff for working professionals. This is an absence-of-evidence judgement.

### Cited Findings
- Career Dreamer lists no jobs and is a one-off exploration (see section 1).
- Study notebooks track progress, but for coursework and exams (see section 1).
- Memory is broad personalisation, user can toggle but not documented as structured goals (see section 4).
- Skills badges and certificates are catalogue-bound; employer consortium recognition (UK, 2022) is the only human/employer bridge found.

### Inferences
- Goal tracking: no goal/check-in model in any Google consumer product found.
- Evidence of progress: only inside study notebooks and course platforms.
- Persona/lens: Gems allow custom instructions (known feature, not re-verified here) but no mentoring methodology.
- Human mentor handoff: none found; no Google equivalent to ADPList/MentorCruise.
- UK context: Career Dreamer US-only (Lightcast/BLS data); UK labour-market data and UK qualification frameworks absent from Google's mentoring-adjacent tools. Employer consortium is the one UK element.
- Risk: sycophancy findings in the existing report apply to Gemini too; Google's own caveat on over-personalisation is the only acknowledgement found.

### Gaps
- Gems' current memory behaviour, Gemini scheduled actions as follow-up mechanism, and any 2026 Google career-coaching launch could have been missed; search coverage was around 16 queries and no full pages read.
