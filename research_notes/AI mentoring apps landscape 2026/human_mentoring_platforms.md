# Human and hybrid mentoring platforms and programme software (state as of Oct 2026)

Method caveat: WebFetch failed on all vendor domains (DNS errors), so findings come from WebSearch result summaries and cited pages, not full-page reads. Much pricing comes from third-party aggregators or competitor blogs (MentorCruise, Qooper and Together all publish "alternatives" posts), so it is flagged as unverified. Few source dates were visible; undated sources are marked.

## Which platforms lead, and what is each one's model, users, pricing, scale?

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) free/low-cost marketplaces (ADPList, GrowthMentor, MentorCruise, Codementor), (b) B2B programme software sold per programme or flat annual licence (Chronus, Together, Mentorloop, PushFar, Guider, Qooper, Ten Thousand Coffees, Mentor Collective), and (c) general platforms with AI coaching (LinkedIn). Several programme vendors publish no prices; third-party figures conflict.

### Cited Findings

Marketplaces
- ADPList: claims 40,000+ verified mentors in 140+ countries (stated current as of Aug 2026); its 2026 State of Mentorship report (April 2026) cites 1,000,000+ sessions. Free for mentees, no premium tier, mentors are unpaid volunteers. Also offers an MCP server giving AI assistants/dev tools programmatic mentor search. — [ADPList](https://adplist.org/state-of-mentorship); [ADPList AI](https://adplist.org/ai). Third-party Stork lists ~36,000 mentors / 160+ countries (conflicts, aggregator) — [Stork](https://www.stork.ai/en/adplist)
- MentorCruise: paid marketplace; mentors set monthly subscriptions plus a platform fee. Estimates conflict: ~$70-$260/month (ADPList guide, 2026), $200-$400/month (Flexiple 2025), $200-$500 (MentorCruise's own ChatGPT consulting page). Typically 1-2 calls/month plus async chat. Mentor pool cited as 7,200 (ADPList) or 5,500+ (Flexiple). Risk-free trial, no contracts (company claim). — [ADPList guide](https://adplist.org/guides/mentorcruise-alternatives); [Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/reviews/mentorcruise); [MentorCruise AI experts](https://mentorcruise.com/experts/ai)
- GrowthMentor: membership for marketers/startup founders. Third-party lists Light ~$50/mo and Pro ~$75/mo (billed quarterly), Teams from ~$70/user/month; Light includes limited free calls, AI matching, Slack. Mentors can charge but are capped (free until 3 reviews; max $99/hr until 10 reviews; HackerNoon, possibly dated). Many members get employer sponsorship. — [Toolmage/search summary](https://www.toolmage.com/en/tool/growthmentor/); [GrowthMentor pricing](https://www.growthmentor.com/pricing/); [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/top-3-online-mentoring-platforms-for-startups-jos32tp)
- Codementor: marketplace for live programming help and freelance work with vetted developers. No current pricing or AI-specific features found. — [Parse](https://www.parse.gl/brands/codementor-io)
- Plato (engineering-leader mentoring): a third-party directory says it was sunset on 19 Dec 2025 as it pivoted to an AI productivity suite (single unverified source); Dealroom shows ~$5.3M funding. Mentors criticised its model of unpaid mentor time in a VC-funded company. — [TechInterview directory](https://www.techinterview.org/bootcamp_directory/p/plato/); [Pragmatic Engineer](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/quit-mentoring-at-plato/amp/); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/plato_1)

- Likely ADPList leads on reach (free), MentorCruise on paid marketplace breadth, Chronus/Together/Mentorloop in enterprise programme software. Ranking is my judgement, not sourced.

### Gaps

## How is AI used for matching, session prep, summaries and nudges?

### Takeaway
AI is mostly bolted onto programme software for matching, agenda/prompt suggestion, nudges and admin insight. Mentee-facing conversational AI is mostly absent or announced as upcoming.

### Cited Findings
- Mentorloop: AI conversation starters for first meetings, session agendas from past notes/goals, nudges for quiet pairs or overdue actions, survey summaries, an AI co-coordinator "Coco"; participant-facing chatbot described as coming "soon". Mentorloop's blog positions AI as supporting, not replacing, mentors. — [Mentorloop AI approach](https://mentorloop.com/our-approach-to-ai/); [Mentorloop Intelligence](https://mentorloop.com/intelligence/); [Why AI can't replace mentors](https://mentorloop.com/blog/why-ai-cant-replace-mentors/)
- Chronus: AI matching, conversation prompts, alerts when relationships go quiet; customised conversation guides described by one third party as planned. — [Chronus pricing/feature summaries](https://us.fitgap.com/products/chronus-mentoring); [Mentorgain](https://www.mentorgain.com/blog/best-mentorship-software-for-companies)
- Together: nudges and cadence reminders; session prompts (competitor table calls it "prompts only"). — [Mentorgain](https://www.mentorgain.com/blog/best-mentorship-software-for-companies)
- Qooper: AI Suite - admin-reviewed AI-suggested matches, personalised meeting agendas, "AI Mentorship Trainer" for mentors, People Insights for retention/skill gap signals (vendor marketing, undated). — [Qooper blog](https://www.qooper.io/blog/mentoring-platforms-for-large-companies-and-global-organizations)
- Guider: matching on 100+ parameters, claimed 96.5-97.4% accuracy (third-party directories, unverified). — [Ensun](https://ensun.io/company/guider-649d8310b0f949335973e7b4)
- GrowthMentor: AI matching included in Light plan. — [Toolmage](https://www.toolmage.com/en/tool/growthmentor/)
- Ten Thousand Coffees: AI matching core feature. — [UWaterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/news/node/1806)
- ADPList: MCP server for AI-assistant mentor search; AI is mainly a mentor-expertise category. — [ADPList AI](https://adplist.org/ai)
- LinkedIn: AI career coach and expert-persona chatbots (Premium). — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/linkedin-wants-you-to-use-ai-tools-to-find-your-next-job)

### Inferences
- Pattern: AI works at the edges of a human relationship (pairing, prompting, monitoring). Session summaries appear mostly as survey/admin summaries, not automatic session transcripts, in what I found.

### Gaps
- Could not verify launch dates of AI features, nor any PushFar AI features or Together/Chronus AI beyond secondary summaries. Sidekick-type tools not covered by any search.

## Pricing and business models

### Takeaway
Programme vendors charge flat annual licences or participant-band subscriptions (or $5-15 per user per month at Qooper); marketplaces charge mentee subscriptions with a platform fee (MentorCruise) or are free (ADPList). See figures above.

### Cited Findings
- See per-vendor pricing above; Gartner says Together is subscription-based, typically sized by users — [Gartner](https://gcom.pdo.aws.gartner.com/reviews/product/together). Chronus reviewers report long implementation/ROI timelines — [G2](https://g2.com/products/chronus-mentoring/pricing).

### Inferences
- Because AI is bundled rather than priced separately (no separate AI pricing found for Together, PushFar or others), vendors are using AI as differentiation not a new revenue line.

### Gaps
- Marketplace take rates not found.

## Gaps these leave for an always-available AI mentor

### Takeaway
Gaps come from cost, scarce human time and AI limited to admin tasks, but evidence is mostly inference.

### Cited Findings
- ADPList mentors are unpaid volunteers and Plato's mentors publicly complained of unpaid labour — [ADPList](https://adplist.org/state-of-mentorship); [Pragmatic Engineer](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/quit-mentoring-at-plato/amp/)
- MentorCruise typically gives 1-2 calls per month at roughly $70-$500/month — [ADPList guide](https://adplist.org/guides/mentorcruise-alternatives)
- Mentorloop's participant chatbot is only "coming soon" — [Mentorloop AI approach](https://mentorloop.com/our-approach-to-ai/)
- Chronus buyers report ~4 months to implement — [G2](https://g2.com/products/chronus-mentoring/pricing)
- Programme tools require organisational purchase at ~$3k-$20k+/yr, with individuals served only by marketplaces — pricing sources above

### Inferences
- Between sessions (1-2 calls per month), a mentee has no always-on support; B2B tools are bought by HR, not individuals; the enterprise tools' AI serves admins, and mentee-facing AI chat is sparse; individuals who are not employees or students are served by pricey marketplaces or volunteer-dependent ADPList. Hybrid positioning ("AI supports humans", like Mentorloop) means an AI-first product can claim the always-on, low-cost slot, while ADPList's MCP server suggests human marketplaces may feed AI agents.
- Human-mentor scarcity is plausible but I did not find outcome studies.

### Gaps
- No programme-outcome research or review sentiment (G2/Trustpilot) collected; no UK-specific market data beyond PushFar and Guider.
