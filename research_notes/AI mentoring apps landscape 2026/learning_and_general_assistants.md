# AI tutoring, career-development and general assistants vs. a dedicated mentoring app (2026)

Research limits: only web-search snippets were used (no page fetches), so most findings are secondary sources and several conflict. Today is 2026-10-14. Items from sources older than about Oct 2025 are flagged [OLD]. Vendor pricing pages were not read directly; all prices need verification.

## General assistants: memory, personas, coaching-style use

### Takeaway
ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all now have cross-chat memory plus a project or persona container. Memory is summary or preference based and user-controllable, not a structured goal or skill model. Gemini Gems reportedly do not use long-term memory. Coaching-style use works through prompts, but none of them proactively tracks goals, schedules check-ins or shows progress analytics. Evidence on Copilot is thin and I found nothing on Pi.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT Study Mode launched July 2025 for Free, Plus, Pro and Team users, with Socratic-style tutoring. It reportedly uses memory of past conversations for continuity. [OLD, July 2025] — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/chatgpt-gets-study-mode-to-tutor-students-with-socratic-prompts-77777.htm); [BYU guide](https://ai.byu.edu/chatgpt-study-mode)
- ChatGPT project-only memory (launched Aug 2025) keeps what is learned inside a project out of account-wide memory. Existing unshared projects can be switched to project-only in project settings. This is described in a guide dated Aug 2026 and works across plans. — [memx.app](https://memx.app/blog/chatgpt-project-only-memory-control/)
- Sources conflict on whether global memory flows into projects and on the date Projects launched (one says Nov 2025). Treat both as unconfirmed. — [Unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/how-to-use-chatgpts-project-memory/); [memx.app](https://memx.app/blog/chatgpt-project-only-memory-control/)
- Claude memory was extended to free users. Memx dates this to about March 2026, but other reports give no date, so the date is unconfirmed. Memory can be paused, reset or bypassed with incognito chats. The memory summary is on all plans, while "search past chats" is paid. — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/ai/anthropic-brings-memory-to-claudes-free-plan-220729070.html); [Memx glossary](https://memx.app/glossary/claude-memory/)
- Claude Projects and Artifacts were made free (undated in the snippet). The Yahoo version of the report lists up to 20 files per chat. [Likely OLD, 2024] — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/claude-just-made-two-of-its-best-features-free-heres-how-to-use-projects-and-artifacts)
- Claude has project-scoped memory for paid users. [OLD, 2025] — [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/anthropic-brings-project-based-memory-to-paid-claude-users/)
- Gemini "personal context" launched Aug 2025. It learns from past chats and is tied to the Google Account. [OLD, Aug 2025] — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/08/13/gemini-personal-context/)
- An Android Authority review says Gems have no long-term memory and only hold the context given at setup. The suggested workaround is a pinned conversation. The article is older, so Gems may have changed. [OLD] — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/gemini-gems-3643778/)
- One secondary guide says explicit saved info is broadly available (including free), but deeper automatic personalization needs a paid Google AI plan. Weak source. — [memx.app Gemini](https://memx.app/blog/gemini-memory-personal-context-explained/)
- Microsoft 365 Copilot memory and personalization (Message Center MC1158329, last updated 2026-06-08) personalizes replies from past chats and work data. It has a page to manage memories, admin controls carry over, and general availability slipped to July 2026. — [mc.merill.net](https://mc.merill.net/message/MC1158329/compare/2026-04-17T23-33-44-15Z/2026-06-08T23-05-44-133Z); [itelio](https://www.itelio.com/en/microsoft-message-center/mc1158329)

### Inferences
- All three major assistants offer persistent but unstructured memory. A mentoring app's differentiators would be explicit goals, progress state, proactive nudges, and portability or ownership of the mentee profile. These gaps are inferred from the absence of such features in the sources, not confirmed by vendor statements.
- Memory is opaque and user-managed, so there is no auditable skill map or goal history. Enterprise Copilot memory is governed by admin policy, which matters for workplace mentoring.
- Gemini Guided Learning, Pi, and Copilot consumer personas could not be verified. Treat any claims about them as unsupported.

### Gaps
- No official OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Microsoft docs or release notes were fetched; the snippets are mostly secondary blogs.
- Nothing found on Gemini Guided Learning, consumer Copilot (as opposed to M365), custom GPTs, or Pi/Inflection status in 2026.
- No current plan pricing for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot was retrieved.
- No Reddit or Hacker News discussion was retrieved.

## Learning and career platforms (courses, skills, interview prep)

### Takeaway
Course and skills platforms add AI tutors on top of content catalogs. The ones with real skill measurement are Pluralsight (Iris and skill assessments) and DataCamp (assessment, career tracks). Enterprise platforms (Degreed, Docebo, Cornerstone, 360Learning) own skills graphs and analytics, but are sold to employers. Interview-prep tools cover a narrow job-search slice. Most pricing is only available from third-party sources.

### Cited Findings
- Pluralsight Iris: AI assistant announced July 30, 2024, which recommends paths, courses, labs and assessments. A separate release (April 15, 2026) added Iris Guided Learning prompts and an expanded AI sandbox with more models. The Iris page lists 500+ skill assessments and 3,500+ hands-on scenarios. Free skill assessments (about 20 questions, 5 minutes) were announced, but the repost is undated. — [SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/ai/pluralsight-adds-new-ai-assistant-to-platform-to-speed-up-users-learning-journeys/); [Bolsamania press release](https://www.bolsamania.com/nota-de-prensa_print/mercados/pluralsight-launches-new-ai-sandbox-guided-learning-and-enterprise-integrations-to-help-teams-build-job-ready-tech-skills--22293493.html); [Pluralsight Iris](https://www.pluralsight.com/lp/iris); [paulwriter repost](https://paulwriter.com/pluralsight-launches-iris-to-measure-and-verify-technology-skills/)
- DataCamp: Premium listed at $28/month billed annually (undated snapshot). A 50% off annual promo ran Oct 5-31, 2026. It covers 790+ courses, Career and Skill Tracks, certifications, projects and assessments. The AI tutor asks who you are and your goal before a lesson (promo copy). A 5-minute assessment gives a level and recommendations. Career tracks include Data Engineer, AI Engineer and others. — [DataCamp pricing](https://www.datacamp.com/pricing?tab=learn); [DataCamp promo](https://www.datacamp.com/promo/odp-oct-26); [Nibble review](https://nibble-app.com/blog/is-datacamp-worth-it)
- Coursera Plus: aggregators disagree ($59/month or $399/year versus about $64/month, July 2026). No evidence was found on whether Coach is included or separately priced. Low reliability. — [Subger](https://subger.com/hu/service/coursera-plus); [i-programmer](https://www.i-programmer.info/news/150-training-a-education/18299-acquire-new-skills-with-coursera-plus.html)
- LinkedIn Learning: aggregator prices conflict ($39.99/month standalone, about $19.99/month on annual, $29.99 elsewhere; Business from $379.88/user/year). AI coaching and role-play are said to have arrived 2024-2025, but whether they are included is not stated. Low reliability. — [Subger](https://subger.com/en/service/linkedin-learning); [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/linkedin-linkedin-learning/pricing)
- O'Reilly Answers: a generative AI search or chatbot trained only on O'Reilly content (powered by Miso), citing sources. It is bundled in the Individual plan, which has 60,000+ books and videos plus labs. Vendr reports enterprise deals of $4,990-$68,419/year (median $20,380, n=73). [OLD, June 2024 launch] — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240611293396/en/OReilly-Unveils-New-Generative-AI-Powered-Search-Engine-Capabilities-to-Enhance-Learning-in-the-Flow-of-Work); [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/buyer-guides/oreilly)

- Pramp is now part of Exponent. Pramp offers 5 free peer mocks per month. Exponent bundle pricing is quoted as $79/month or about $12/month annual (conflicting, unverified). Interviewing.io is human-led by FAANG engineers: $179-$300+ per session, with a $2,000 three-session package. — [techinterview.org](https://www.techinterview.org/post/3233474681/mock-interview-platforms/?format=md); [Lodely](https://www.lodely.com/blog/interviewing-io-pricing); [scale.jobs](https://scale.jobs/blog/best-mock-interview-platforms-tech-jobs)

Degreed: AI skills mapping, personalized paths, "Maestro Studio", quote-based pricing. Cornerstone: Skills Graph, possible 2026 rebrand to "Galaxy" (single source). 360Learning: peer-authored learning, AI to find SMEs and learner sticking points. Prices conflict ($8-10/user/month versus custom quotes). — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-corporate-training-2026); [AimadeFor](https://www.aimadefor.com/blog/ai-learning-development-platforms-hr/); [Docebo blog](https://www.docebo.com/learning-network/blog/docebo-sanalabs-workday/) (competitor marketing)

### Inferences
- Pluralsight and DataCamp are the closest to a skill map for technical roles. Sana, Degreed, Docebo and Cornerstone are employer-purchased, so an individual engineer cannot easily use them.
- Interview-prep tools are episodic and transactional, with no long-term mentee model.
- Consumer course AI (Coach, O'Reilly Answers) is scoped to the vendor's own catalog, which is a differentiator versus general assistants but is not coaching.

### Gaps
- No usable information found on Coursera Coach features, Khanmigo (features or price), Duolingo Max (prices in sources conflict: roughly $9.99-$19.99/month), Kira Learning, Exponent AI features, or LinkedIn's AI coach details.
- No traction data (user counts, revenue) other than Sana's and Vendr's O'Reilly figures.
- Docebo, Degreed, Cornerstone and 360Learning claims come from vendor-comparison blogs, not official docs.
- Nothing retrieved from Reddit/HN or independent analysts (e.g. Josh Bersin).

## Which tools offer goal tracking, long-term memory, skill maps or progress analytics

### Takeaway
Based on what I could verify: skill assessments and maps exist at Pluralsight, DataCamp and the enterprise suites; long-term conversational memory exists only in the general assistants; no source showed a product combining both for individual technical professionals.

### Cited Findings
- Long-term memory: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (not Gems), and M365 Copilot, as cited above.
- Skill assessment and levels: Pluralsight (500+ assessments), DataCamp (5-minute assessment, tracks). See the sources in the previous section.
- Skills graphs or maps and analytics at enterprise scale: Degreed, Docebo, Cornerstone, per vendor-comparison blogs ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-corporate-training-2026)).

### Inferences
- A whitespace likely exists for an individual-owned mentoring product combining persistent memory with an explicit skill and goal model for data and software engineers. This is inference only and needs validation.

### Gaps
- Goal-tracking features of Coursera, LinkedIn and Khanmigo were not verified.
- Feature-by-feature comparison not possible without vendor docs.
