# Building the evidence for a move to staff engineer

Moving from senior to staff is mostly about scope and influence rather than doing the same work better.

## What changes

Senior engineers deliver well on problems handed to them. Staff engineers find and frame the right problems, work across teams, and make others more effective. Technical depth still matters, but so do communication, judgement and the ability to build agreement.

## Evidence to collect

Projects where you set direction across several teams, with the outcome. Decisions you drove and the reasoning, such as design documents and architecture decision records. People you have grown, and how. Changes to standards or practice that others adopted. Problems you prevented.

## How to work on it

Agree with your manager what the next level looks like in your organisation, because definitions vary. Ask for a sponsor who sees work at that scope. Keep a running record of achievements with dates, impact and links, and review it every quarter.

## Be realistic

Promotion depends on need and timing as well as readiness. If the role does not exist where you are, discuss whether it can, or whether the move is elsewhere.
