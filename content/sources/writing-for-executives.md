# Writing for executives: lead with the decision

Senior readers have little time and many demands. They want to know what you need from them and why, then enough detail to trust it.

## Structure

Start with the point: the recommendation or the decision required. Follow with the two or three reasons that matter most. State the options you considered and the risks. Put detail in an appendix.

## Language

Use plain words and short sentences. Explain technical terms or avoid them. Give numbers with units and context, such as cost per year compared with the current cost. Be explicit about what is a fact, what is an estimate and what is a judgement.

## Make the ask clear

Say what you want, by when, and what happens if nothing is decided. One clear request is better than several.

## Check before sending

Could someone understand the main point from the first two sentences? Is every claim one you could defend? Would you be comfortable if this were forwarded?
